Chapter One, The Skeletal System

Finished

There are many different types of bones.

The two groups of bones

  • Axial bones: the bones that connect to the middle of the body — 80 of them.
  • Appendicular bones: the bones in the arms and legs — 126 of them.
  • There are 206 bones in all.

The skeletal system helps the body function in many ways. Without your bones you would not be stable. The red blood cells provide the circulatory system gives oxygen all over the body. For an example, all your organs depend on oxygen. That's what makes the skeletal system so helpful.

What bones do

  • Bones protect your organs.
  • Bones support your body weight.
  • Bones produce osteocalcin, which helps regulate metabolism and blood sugar.
  • Bones store calcium and iron for metabolism.
  • There are three layers of bone.
  • The periosteum is the layer that has cartilage.
  • Bones also have collagen.

The skeletal system helps our body shape and oxygen; without the skeletal system, we would not be alive.

Chapter Two, The Muscular System

Finished

In this chapter you will learn how many muscles are in your body, the three different types of muscles, what muscles do and what would happen without them.

There are three different types of muscle in your body which each has a different job.

The three types of muscles

  • Voluntary muscles are the muscles that you can control.
  • Involuntary muscles are the one that you cannot control.
  • Cardiac muscle: the muscle in your heart — the one that never stops. One cardiac muscle: the heart.

Vihaan's count: about 750 muscles.

Muscles help you with day to day activities.

What happens without them

  • Without muscles your body would not work properly.
  • Without your voluntary muscles you would not be able to move or eat.
  • Without your involuntary muscles, you couldn't digest food or breathe.

In conclusion, the muscular system helps you do day to day activities without the muscular system you would not be alive.
