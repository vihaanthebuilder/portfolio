    📝 My Game Review (Rapid Games)

    The big things my coach showed me when we looked at my games together.

    • ♘I moved the same knight back and forth in the opening — that wastes time. Develop all my pieces!
    • ⚠️Taking on g5 was a trap, and Bxd4 lost material — Bg4 would have kept me safe.
    • ♛In the Scandinavian (1.e4 d5), bringing my queen out early gave away tempo.
    • 🛡️When my opponent attacks my king (threats on h2), defend in time — f4 was the best move there.
    • 🏰I missed free rooks more than once! Always scan for pieces I can just grab.
    • 🚫Moves like a6 and b3 had no plan — every move should develop or make a threat.
    • ⏱️In 30-minute games, take 20–30 seconds a move to calculate before I play.
    • ✂️Use tactics like forks to win material and trade down when I'm ahead.
    • 🤝Know when it's a draw — a messy or equal spot can end in threefold repetition.
